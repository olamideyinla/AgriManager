## 2. The Problem We Solve

Most smallholder farmers across Africa, Asia, and Latin America run their operations on memory, handwritten notebooks, or WhatsApp voice notes. This leads to five expensive problems:

| Problem | What It Costs the Farmer |
|---|---|
| No daily records | Cannot calculate real profit per flock, pond, or harvest season |
| Manual feed tracking | Overbuying, wastage, and inability to catch theft early |
| Payroll done by hand | Errors, disputes, and hours lost every salary month |
| No financial overview | Cannot separate personal money from farm money; banks won't lend |
| No health history | Vaccination gaps lead to disease outbreaks and sudden loss |

These are not small inconveniences. A poultry farmer with 1,000 birds who can't track mortality, feed consumption, and egg production accurately could be losing ₦50,000–₦150,000 per flock cycle without knowing it.

**AgriManagerX eliminates all five problems with a single app.**

## 3. What AgriManagerX Is

AgriManagerX is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — it works like a native mobile app but installs from a browser. No app store required. No large download. Works on any Android or iOS smartphone.

**Three things make it unique:**

### 3.1 Offline First
The app works **fully without internet**. All data is stored on the device. When the phone connects to the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data), everything syncs automatically to the cloud. This matters enormously in rural areas with unstable connectivity.

### 3.2 Built Specifically for African Farm Realities
- Currency support including ₦ (NGN), KES, GHS, UGX, ZAR, and others
- Farm types that matter locally: layers, broilers, cattle, fish ponds, crop farms, goats, pigs, rabbits
- Payroll designed for daily-wage and monthly-wage farm workers
- Invoicing that generates professional documents shareable directly via WhatsApp

### 3.3 Role-Based Team Access
A farm is not one person. AgriManagerX supports multiple users per farm with five access levels:

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Full access — setup, financials, team management, all reports |
| Manager | Operations — daily entries, inventory, labor, financials |
| Supervisor | Oversee workers, view records, assign tasks |
| Worker | Submit daily records and assigned tasks only |
| Viewer | Read-only — for investors, accountants, or extension officers |

## 4. Every Feature, Explained

### 4.1 Daily Production Tracking
The core of the app. Farm workers record the day's activity in under 60 seconds.

**Layers (Egg Production)**
- Eggs collected, broken eggs, rejected eggs
- Mortality count and cause
- Feed consumed (kg) and feed type
- Water consumed (litres)
- Temperature (high/low)
- Automatic calculation: Hen Day Production % (HDP), cumulative mortality %, feed cost per tray

**Broilers (Meat Birds)**
- Daily mortality and cause
- Feed consumed and feed type
- Body weight sample (average and sample size)
- Automatic calculation: Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR), mortality %, projected slaughter weight vs Ross 308 standard curve

**Cattle**
- Milk yield (litres) and milking count per day
- Feed consumed and feed type
- Deaths and births
- Health notes
- Automatic calculation: daily yield trend, herd size changes

**Fish Ponds**
- Feed given (kg) and feed type
- Estimated mortality
- Water quality: temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, ammonia levels
- Automatic calculation: survival rate %, feed trend, water quality status (safe / warning / danger)

**Crops**
- Activity type: land preparation, planting, fertilising, spraying, weeding, harvesting
- Inputs used: name, quantity, and unit
- Labour: hours worked and worker count
- Harvest: quantity (kg) and grade
- Growth stage and pest/disease notes
- Weather notes

**Other Livestock (Pigs, Goats, Rabbits, Custom)**
- Mortality, feed consumption, body weight samples
- Configurable for any livestock type not listed above

---

### 4.2 Enterprise Management
An **enterprise** is one active production unit — one flock, one pond, one crop season, one herd. AgriManagerX tracks each enterprise separately so profit and loss are always clear.

- Set up multiple enterprises at once (e.g. House A Layers + House B Broilers + Fishpond 1)
- Each enterprise has its own dashboard, production curve, financial summary, and health log
- Close an enterprise when a flock is sold or a crop season ends; open a new one immediately
- Compare performance across batches: previous flocks vs current flock side by side

---

### 4.3 Financial Ledger
Every naira in and out of the farm is recorded and sorted automatically.

**Sales recording:**
- Sell eggs, live birds, fish, milk, crops, or any farm produce
- Auto-links to the relevant enterprise
- Calculates revenue per unit sold

**Expense recording:**
- Feed, medicine, utilities, transport, labour, maintenance, miscellaneous
- Categorised automatically for clean profit/loss reports

**Profit & Loss per enterprise:**
- Gross revenue minus all costs = net profit per flock, per pond, per season
- No accounting knowledge required — the app calculates everything

**Accounts Receivable:**
- Track credit sales — who owes money and how much
- Mark payments when received

---

### 4.4 Inventory & Stock Control
Never run out of feed unexpectedly again.

- Add all inventory items: feed types, vaccines, medications, fuel, equipment
- Record stock-in when buying (includes unit cost for average cost tracking)
- Stock-out is logged automatically when daily feed entries are recorded — no double entry
- **Low-stock alerts:** the app calculates average daily consumption and warns the farmer when stock will run out within a set number of days
- **Stock adjustments:** record physical counts and reconcile discrepancies
- Full movement history per item: every receipt, usage, and adjustment is logged

---

### 4.5 Labor & Payroll
One of the most powerful features for farms with employees.

**Worker Management:**
- Add permanent and casual workers
- Assign workers to specific farm locations or enterprises

**Attendance & Daily Labor:**
- Record daily attendance for permanent workers
- Log casual labor by day and task (with daily rate)

**Automated Payroll:**
- At the end of the month, run payroll in one click
- The app calculates gross pay from attendance, deducts configured taxes and allowances, and generates individual payslips
- Payslips are printable and shareable via WhatsApp or PDF
- Payroll history is stored and searchable

**Payroll Settings:**
- Configure pay frequency, default deductions (PAYE, pension, etc.), allowances, and currency per farm

---

### 4.6 Health Schedule & Alerts
Stay ahead of disease and never miss a vaccination.

**Health Schedule:**
- Set up vaccination and medication protocols per enterprise type
- The app generates a health calendar with due dates
- Mark events as complete with dosage and batch records

**Automatic Alerts:**
The system monitors farm data and flags issues before they become crises:

| Alert Type | Example |
|---|---|
| High mortality spike | "Mortality rate exceeded 3% today in House A Broilers" |
| Low egg production | "HDP has dropped below 70% for 3 consecutive days" |
| Feed stock critical | "Layers feed will run out in 2 days at current consumption rate" |
| Vaccination due | "Newcastle Disease booster due in 3 days for House B Layers" |
| Weight below standard | "Average broiler weight is 15% below Ross 308 standard at Day 28" |
| Water quality warning | "Pond pH recorded outside safe range (8.9 — expected 6.5–8.5)" |

Alerts appear in-app and can trigger push notifications on the farmer's phone.

## 6. Who Should Be Using This App

### Primary Targets (Highest Conversion Probability)

**1. Commercial Poultry Farmers (500+ birds)**
These farmers are already keeping some records. They understand the concept of tracking — they just do it manually. The shift to digital is natural. Feed cost alone justifies the subscription: a 1% improvement in FCR on 1,000 broilers can save ₦30,000–₦50,000 per cycle.

**2. Fish Farmers with 2+ Ponds**
Water quality management and feeding efficiency directly impact survival rates. Farmers who've lost a pond to ammonia spikes or overfeeding are immediately receptive.

**3. Farms with More Than 3 Workers**
Payroll automation alone is worth the Pro subscription for any farm with more than three employees. Monthly payroll done manually takes hours and creates disputes. AgriManagerX does it in minutes.

**4. Cooperative Farms or Nucleus Farms Managing Multiple Units**
Role-based access allows the owner, manager, and workers to each use the app with appropriate permissions. This is ideal for organized cooperatives.

### Secondary Targets (Strong Growth Potential)

**5. Crop Farmers with Hired Labor**
Labor cost tracking and harvest records are the hooks. Farmers who grow maize, cassava, vegetables, or tomatoes with seasonal workers benefit immediately from labor tracking and payroll.

**6. Mixed Enterprise Farms**
A farm with layers, a fishpond, and a small crop operation can manage everything in one place instead of separate notebooks.

**7. Farms Seeking Finance or Insurance**
Banks and microfinance institutions increasingly ask for farm records before lending. AgriManagerX generates the kind of structured financial reports that satisfy lenders. This is a powerful pitch angle.

### Who Is NOT the Right Target (Yet)

- Subsistence farmers with no cash income — cannot afford even the free tier's smartphone requirement
- Farmers who have never kept any records and are strongly resistant — require more education first
- Very large commercial farms with dedicated ERP systems — AgriManagerX is built for the $0–$50K revenue tier

No. The free plan is truly free forever. The Pro trial is 30 days with no credit card required.

Yes (Pro plan). An owner on one phone and a manager on another phone can both access the same farm data simultaneously.

Yes. Pro subscribers have access to email support and WhatsApp support. The partner (you) is also a first-line support resource for basic questions.
