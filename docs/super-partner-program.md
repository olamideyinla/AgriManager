## 1. Welcome to the Super Partner Program

Congratulations on your advancement within the AgriManagerX Partner Program. As a **Super Partner**, you have demonstrated the relationship-building ability and sales consistency that qualifies you to lead a team of Partners beneath you.

The Super Partner Program is the next level above the standard Commission Sales Partner. You retain all your existing Partner earnings while gaining a new, passive income stream: a **10% override commission** on every commission generated by the Partners you recruit into your network.

This document outlines everything you need to know about your expanded role, your earnings structure, your toolkit, and your responsibilities as a network leader.

---

## 2. How the Super Partner Model Works

As a Super Partner, you operate on two levels simultaneously:

### Level 1 — Your Own Direct Referrals
Everything from the standard Partner Program continues unchanged:
- You share your referral link with farmers
- Farmers subscribe to AgriManagerX Pro
- You earn 20% on new Pro subscriptions and 10% on renewals
- Volume bonuses still apply based on your own referral count

### Level 2 — Your Sub-Partner Network
In addition to your direct earnings:
- You receive a **unique recruitment link** (distinct from your referral link)
- Any Partner who applies through your recruitment link and is approved becomes your **sub-partner**
- Every time a sub-partner earns a commission, you automatically receive **10% of that commission** as an override
- This applies to all commission types — new subscriptions, renewals, and volume bonuses

There is no limit to the number of sub-partners you can recruit. Your override earnings scale with the size and performance of your network.

## 3. Recruitment Code & Link

Upon promotion to Super Partner, you receive a unique **recruitment code** automatically generated from your name.

**Format:** First six letters of your name + two-digit code
**Example:** If your name is John Olawale → `JOHNOL-42`

**Your recruitment link:**
```
agrimanagerx.com/partners/apply?via=JOHNOL-42
```

This link is separate from your referral link (which you use to refer farmers). Use your **recruitment link exclusively** when inviting other people to join the Partner Program under your network.

**Important:** Do not share your referral link and recruitment link interchangeably. Farmers use the referral link to subscribe to AgriManagerX. Prospective Partners use the recruitment link to apply to the Partner Program.

## 4. Commission Structure

### 4.1 Your Direct Commissions (Unchanged from Standard Partner)

| Sale Type | Your Commission |
|---|---|
| Pro Monthly ($9/mo) | 30% = $2.70 per month per active subscriber |
| Pro Annual ($86/yr) | 30% = $25.80 upfront per subscription sold |
| Renewal (Year 2+) | 20% recurring as long as the farmer stays subscribed |

### 4.2 Volume Bonuses (Unchanged — Based on Your Own Referrals)

| Active Pro Subscribers You Referred | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 10 – 24 | +5% on all your new sales that quarter |
| 25 – 49 | +10% on all your new sales that quarter + "Silver Partner" badge |
| 50+ | +15% on all your new sales that quarter + "Gold Partner" badge + priority support |

### 4.3 Override Commissions (Super Partner Exclusive)

| Sub-Partner's Commission Type | Your Override |
|---|---|
| Sub-partner earns commission on new Pro Monthly | 10% of their commission |
| Sub-partner earns commission on new Pro Annual | 10% of their commission |
| Sub-partner earns a renewal commission | 10% of their commission |

The override is calculated on the **net commission amount** credited to the sub-partner — not on the subscription price paid by the farmer.

**Override commissions are automatic.** No action is required from you. When a sub-partner's commission is approved, your override is generated instantly.

### 4.4 Earnings Example

> **Your direct referrals this month:**
> You refer 4 farmers on Pro Monthly → 4 × $2.70 = **$10.80**
>
> **Your network this month:**
> You have 6 active sub-partners. Each refers 2 farmers on Pro Monthly.
> Each sub-partner earns 2 × $2.70 = $5.40
> Your 10% override per sub-partner: $0.54
> Total override from 6 sub-partners: 6 × $0.54 = **$3.24**
>
> **Your total earnings this month: $10.80 + $3.24 = $14.04**
>
> As your network grows, override income grows passively — even in months when your own direct referrals are low.

## 5. Sub-Partner Responsibilities

As a Super Partner, you are a leader in your network. While you are not legally responsible for your sub-partners' actions, your success is tied to their performance. We encourage you to:

- **Onboard them properly** — Share this document and the standard Partner Program document with every sub-partner you recruit
- **Give them the pitch** — Forward the WhatsApp script and demo walk-through guide
- **Check in regularly** — A simple WhatsApp message asking how their demos are going goes a long way
- **Escalate issues** — If a sub-partner is struggling, connect them with our support team

Sub-partners operate fully independently. They set their own hours, manage their own territories, and receive all standard Partner benefits directly from AgriManagerX. You do not manage their payouts or access their personal dashboard.

## 7. Recruiting Sub-Partners: Step by Step

### Step 1 — Identify Candidates
Look for people with strong agricultural networks who are not yet AgriManagerX Partners. Strong candidates include:
- Fellow agrovets, feed dealers, or input suppliers in other territories
- Extension officers in neighboring districts
- Members of your cooperative who interact with many small farms
- Agricultural graduates looking for income opportunities
- Field officers at NGOs working in agriculture

### Step 2 — Share Your Recruitment Link
Send them a message such as:

> *"I earn recurring monthly income by helping farmers sign up for an agri-management app — AgriManagerX. I've been a Partner for [X months] and it's been worthwhile. If you'd like to join under my team, apply here: agrimanagerx.com/partners/apply?via=YOURCODE. No cost to apply. Approved in 3 business days."*

### Step 3 — Help Them Through Onboarding
Once they apply and are approved, share:
- The standard Partner Program document
- The WhatsApp pitch script
- The demo walk-through guide
- Invite them to your own WhatsApp group for your network (optional but recommended)

### Step 4 — Monitor Their Progress
Use your Network dashboard tab to track:
- Which sub-partners have been active (made referrals)
- Who has not yet made their first referral (reach out and offer help)
- Your running total override commissions per sub-partner

### Step 5 — Keep Growing
There is no cap on network size. Recruiting 10 active sub-partners who each refer just 5 farmers per month can generate more passive override income than your direct referrals alone.

## 9. Payment Terms

Override commissions are processed on the same schedule as direct commissions:

- Calculated on the **1st of each month** for the previous month's approved commissions
- Paid by the **15th of each month** via:
  - Bank transfer (NG, KE, GH, and others)
  - Mobile money (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money)
  - PayPal (where available)
- Minimum payout threshold: **$20** (override + direct commissions combined). Balances below $20 roll over.
- Monthly statements show your direct earnings and override earnings separately.

Override commissions are credited to your wallet when the corresponding sub-partner commission moves to **Approved** status. If a sub-partner commission is reversed (e.g. subscription cancelled before the month ends), the associated override is also reversed.

## 10. Super Partner Terms & Conditions

### 10.1 Eligibility
To be eligible for Super Partner status:
- You must be an active AgriManagerX Partner in Good Standing (not suspended or under review)
- You must have been an active partner for a minimum of 30 days
- Promotion is granted at AgriManagerX's discretion based on performance and territory need

### 10.2 What You May Do
- Everything permitted under the standard Partner Agreement
- Recruit sub-partners using your recruitment link
- Build and manage a network of sub-partners
- Create a private WhatsApp or Telegram group for your network
- Use the "Super Partner" designation in personal communications and pitch materials

### 10.3 What You May Not Do
- Represent AgriManagerX Super Partner status as a franchise or business ownership arrangement
- Charge sub-partners any fee to join your network
- Promise sub-partners specific earnings amounts or guaranteed income
- Create any sub-partner agreement or contract independently of AgriManagerX
- Share your recruitment link in paid advertising without prior written approval

### 10.4 No Pyramid or Multi-Level Structure
The AgriManagerX Super Partner Program is a **single-level override** model. Sub-partners do not earn overrides on other sub-partners. The chain is:

```
AgriManagerX → Super Partner (override) → Sub-Partner (direct commissions) → Farmer (subscriber)
```

Sub-partners who perform exceptionally well may themselves be eligible for Super Partner promotion in the future, at which point they would build their own independent network. You do not earn from their network.

### 10.5 Demotion
Super Partner status may be revoked if:
- Your account is suspended for a terms violation
- Your network is found to contain partners recruited through misrepresentation
- You have been inactive (no referrals and no active sub-partners) for 90 consecutive days

If demoted, you revert to standard Partner status. Pending override commissions earned before demotion are paid in full.

### 10.6 Termination
All terms of the standard Partner Agreement's termination clause apply. Override commissions on your sub-partners' sales continue for **12 months** after termination, after which the sub-partners are reassigned to AgriManagerX's direct management.

### 10.7 Confidentiality
All terms from the standard Partner Agreement's confidentiality clause apply to this document as well.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does it take to get promoted to Super Partner?**
There is no fixed timeline. Submit a request via your partner portal or by emailing partners@agrimanagerx.com after you have been an active partner for at least 30 days. Our team reviews requests within 5 business days.

**Q: Can I see what my sub-partners are earning?**
No. You can see how much override income each sub-partner has generated for you, but you cannot see their personal earnings, payout history, or dashboard.

**Q: What if someone applies through my recruitment link but is rejected?**
Rejected applicants are not added to your network. You only earn overrides on approved, active sub-partners.

**Q: Can my sub-partners also become Super Partners?**
Yes. If a sub-partner qualifies and is promoted to Super Partner, they build their own independent network. Their promotion has no impact on your override earnings from their direct commissions — you continue to earn overrides on their own referral commissions as before. You do not, however, earn from their new sub-partners.

**Q: What happens if a sub-partner recruits farmers using my referral link instead of their own?**
Commissions go to whoever's referral link was used. If a sub-partner mistakenly uses your referral link, that farmer's subscription is credited to you as a direct referral — not as a sub-partner referral. Instruct your sub-partners to always use their own referral link for farmers.

**Q: Is there a minimum number of sub-partners I need to maintain?**
No. There is no minimum. However, having zero active sub-partners for 90 days may result in a review of your Super Partner status.

**Q: Can I be a sub-partner of another Super Partner and also be a Super Partner myself?**
No. Super Partners operate independently. If you were recruited through another Super Partner before your promotion, your account is transitioned to independent status upon becoming a Super Partner.
